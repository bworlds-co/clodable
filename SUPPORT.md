# Getting help

Clodable is for people who build apps with AI app builders such as Lovable, Bolt or Base44, and most of them aren't developers. You don't need technical words to ask for help.

## First, the troubleshooting page

Start with [Troubleshooting](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md). Your answer may already be there.

## Still stuck? Tell us what happened

Fill in the [Something isn't working](https://github.com/bworlds-co/clodable/issues/new?template=1-something-isnt-working.yml) form. Say what you were trying to do and what you saw: the exact words on screen, or a screenshot.

Questions are welcome there too: it's the one place to ask in public. Because it's public, leave out keys, passwords and your users' data.

## Something private

For anything you'd rather not post in public, email [contact@bworlds.co](mailto:contact@bworlds.co). The maintainer reads it.

Found a security problem? Report it privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) explains.

## Help with a move

Moving an app off its builder works for Lovable apps only, for now. BWorlds, which makes Clodable, offers paid help with moving an app for those who want it. It's optional.
