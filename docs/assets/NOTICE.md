These files in this folder are BWorlds' brand assets. They're not covered by the MIT license at the root of this repository: see [TRADEMARKS.md](../../TRADEMARKS.md).

- `logo.svg`, `favicon.svg`: the clod, Clodable's logo
- `wordmark.svg`, `wordmark-dark.svg`, `lockup.svg`, `lockup-dark.svg`: the Clodable wordmark, alone and beside the logo
- `social-preview.png`: the link preview
- `mascot/`: the mascot's drawings

The screenshots in this folder (`hero.png`, `edit-text.png`, `point-and-ask.png`, `try-to-fix.png`, `locked-text.png`) show the product and follow the repository's license, except for the logo and mascot they contain.
