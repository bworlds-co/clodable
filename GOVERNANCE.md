# Governance

## Who looks after Clodable

Clodable is made by [BWorlds](https://bworlds.co), a small company that helps people own the apps they built with AI. BWorlds maintains it, and the maintainer is [@emmanuelvulk](https://github.com/emmanuelvulk).

Clodable is free and open source, under the [MIT license](LICENSE). It sends BWorlds nothing, and it has no paid tier. BWorlds earns from optional help with moving apps, and from audits.

## How decisions are made

- **Proposals start as an issue.** Describe the problem first, then what you'd change. The [idea form](https://github.com/bworlds-co/clodable/issues/new?template=3-idea.yml) asks in that order.
- **The maintainer decides**, and records each decision as a dated entry in [docs/DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md).
- **Settled decisions change through a new dated entry**, not by rewriting old ones.

## The name, and forks

The code is MIT-licensed, and it stays MIT. BWorlds keeps the name Clodable, its logo and its mascot, the official plugin marketplace (this repository) and the `clodable` package on npm.

Forks are welcome. Please give yours its own name, logo and mascot, so people can tell them apart.
